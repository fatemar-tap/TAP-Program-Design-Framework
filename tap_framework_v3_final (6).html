TAP Team — Programme Team + Curriculum Team

The TAP Team has two distinct functions: the Programme Team (field operations, school relationships, monitoring, government engagement — max 1 per district) and the Curriculum Team (content design, state adaptation, PAL logic, teacher kit production). Both are critical and have distinct responsibilities.

Programme Team — field operations
1 PI per district, 50–100 schools. Reviews exception dashboard only (30 min/week). Acts on auto-flags: zero session logs, inactive students >3 weeks, submission anomalies. Does not visit all schools weekly. Cost constraint
School induction (one-time per school per year). Sets up Teacher PWA, PI+Teacher WA group, Parent-Teacher WA group. Pre-loads class lists. Distributes screen-mirror adaptors. Co-runs first 3 sessions in Mode 1B.
Hierarchical authority cascade. When G1 (Policy Advocate — senior district education officer) aligns: TAP team drafts G1 circular → HMs receive timetable permission → teacher CPD and career progression formally tied to TAP participation → teachers become accountable implementation partners, not volunteers. TAP team owns drafting the G1 circular, preparing HM briefing, and ensuring CPD credit is formally logged. Internal TAP government engagement framework
Quarterly G1/G2 alignment meeting (PI + senior TAP). G1 = Policy Advocate. G2 = Metrics Gatekeeper (district data/quality officer) — needs ASER-comparable data. G3 = Bureaucratic Enabler (cluster coordinator) — needs pre-written forward templates. Programme team owns all three relationships. Internal TAP stakeholder archetype framework
TAP schedule + endorsement via Parent-Teacher WA group. Not printed cards, not individual messages. Programme team provides template; HM/teacher forwards. Scalable, zero per-copy cost.
Teacher conversational help. Teacher doubts resolved via Teacher Help bot (WA-based, conversational) and PI representative on PI+Teacher WA group. Teachers never navigate complex portals alone.
Curriculum Team — content + PAL
Modular state-curriculum plug-in architecture. Every TAP activity has a core version (universal) + state-variant slot. PI collects state board syllabus mapping at quarterly G1 meeting → curriculum team plugs in → school receives aligned content next term. Content for a TAP class period differs from content for the TAP PWA — not the same asset. PRD: modular curriculum requirement
Content differentiated by delivery mode. Mode 1A: projector-optimised, spoken quiz script, OCR-scannable worksheet. Mode 1B: interactive PWA flow, self-paced, auto-quiz. Mode 2 (Hybrid): bite-sized video + PWA activity + submission ladder (emoji → word → photo). Curriculum team designs each variant.
PAL logic. Mode 1A: class-level PAL — content advances when majority of class submits at current level. Mode 1B + 2: individual PAL — quiz score + submission type → PWA adapts difficulty per student. Curriculum team designs differentiated activity variants; system routes automatically. Personalised Exercises RCT 2026 · Mindspark PAL
Teacher cue cards + Period Kit. Shared digitally on PI+Teacher WA group (not printed — scalable, zero cost). Cue cards: 3–4 min max, ready-made, structured to make teacher look successful. Includes: session goal, 2 discussion questions, spoken quiz, subject alignment note.
Nudge template library. Ready-made parent nudge messages, student re-engagement messages, teacher shoutout templates — in regional languages. Shared on WA groups for teachers and parents to forward. Zero per-message cost.
Gamification asset library. Badges, skill gems, streaks, certificates, skill passport (digital + physical), trump cards, level progression. Curriculum team designs; PWA and Teacher PWA render them. Teacher Research 2025 (Collectible cards) · Brazil RCT 2026
Data, monitoring + source notes
Teacher PWA → PI dashboard pipeline. Session logs, quiz score entries, OCR-scanned worksheet data flow from Teacher PWA to PI dashboard automatically. No manual export.
OCR-scannable worksheet (Mode 1A). Curriculum team designs worksheets with machine-readable fields. Teacher scans with phone → data extracted → logged to class record in Teacher PWA. Bridges physical delivery with digital tracking at scale.
Dashboard columns by mode. Mode 1A: session count + submission count (teacher-logged). Mode 1B: in-school PWA completion (auto-logged). Mode 2: linked event — in-school session + home PWA submission → two timestamps, one record. 3-column view: in-school / home / both.
Termly learning trend report (G2-facing). Composite: quiz scores + submission quality + session attendance + PAL progression, mapped to NEP 2020 competencies. This converts G2 endorsement into G1 circular. Mindspark PAL · Blueprints of Progress

Evidence base — comparable programmes + RCTs
Delivery model comparables
Pratham FLN Hybrid (Rajasthan, 2023). In-school card + home PWA → 41% completion vs 18% push-only. School session = social commitment device. Pratham Annual Report 2023
Mindspark PAL — Personalised Adaptive Learning (India, 2025). +0.22 SD gain — only when supervision held. Supervision drop = usage drop = impact drop. In-school anchor not optional. Adapting for Scale 2025
Delhi Happiness Curriculum. 20-min weekly non-exam period, pre-made teacher kit, zero prep. 1,000+ govt schools. The kit IS the programme. Delhi Directorate of Education 2022
UNICEF Learning Passport. PWA-first, 2G-capable, offline sync — marginalised learners in 40 countries. UNICEF Learning Passport 2023
iGrad Tata Trusts (Maharashtra). PWA, offline sync → 70%+ in-school vs 24% after-school push. Facilitated presence doubles completion. Tata Trusts iGrad Study 2023
Teacher motivation RCTs
STiR Education (India/Uganda, 2022). CPD credit + peer networks → 60% higher programme retention vs control. CPD = single most effective teacher incentive in low-resource systems. STiR Education Impact Report 2022
Personalised Exercises RCT (Taiwan, 2026). Weakest students +0.17 SD vs +0.04 SD for strong students. Teacher workload unchanged. Chung et al. 2026
Teach For India low-income schools. In-class anchor + home digital task → highest sustained engagement. Teacher as social contract enforcer. TFI Impact Study 2023
Khan Academy India CBSE pilot. Browser-based, class session login — 0.18 SD facilitated vs 0.04 SD unsupervised. Khan Academy India Pilot 2023
Recognition + social proof RCTs
Brazil RCT — Agency Fund (2026). One class-level award → +4.5% peer participation, +13% top performance from ability-matched classmates. Purely symbolic. Class-level outperforms global leaderboard. Agency Fund Brazil RCT 2026
Pratham Digital (UP/Bihar, 2022). WA group + offline PWA → 34% completion vs 12% bot push. WA group 3× more effective for marginalised cohorts. Pratham Digital Impact Report 2022
ASER 2024. Schools with structured weekly skill period + home follow-up → 2× foundational literacy gain vs after-school only. ASER Annual Status of Education Report 2024
